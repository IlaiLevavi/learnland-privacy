LearnLand — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

LearnLand is a geography quiz game. Single-player works fully offline and needs no account. This policy explains what data the game collects, why, who processes it, the choices you have, and how to delete it.

Who we are

LearnLand is developed by Ilai Levavi. Contact: ilailevavi@gmail.com.

On your device

Your settings, language, scores, coins, levels, stamp album and the record of which countries you have answered are stored in the app's local storage on your phone. They stay there unless a feature below sends them.

Usage data (analytics)

To learn how the game is played and make it better, LearnLand uses Google Analytics for Firebase. You decide whether it runs:

  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom (and when the app cannot tell where your phone is), the game asks first, at the start of the first run. Nothing is collected unless you say yes. If you say no, you are not asked again.
  • Elsewhere, usage data is on by default.
  • Everywhere, you can switch it on or off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data. Switching it off stops collection and deletes the analytics identifier and any not-yet-sent usage data stored on your phone.

When it is on, the game sends:

  • Game events: when a round ends or is quit (the mode, difficulty, number of questions, right answers, score, coins, experience and letters earned, how long the round took, whether it reached its pass mark, and your Explorer Chain before and after it); when a miss puts the pass mark out of reach, and what the game offered; when a second chance is taken (its price in coins and whether it was answered right); when your Explorer Chain ends or a shield keeps it; when a stamp pack is opened (its kind, where it came from and how many new stamps it held) or a star chest is opened (your stars before it); when an album page, the whole album or its gold round is completed (and on which day of the season); when a level is reached, and which new feature a level introduced; when a new feature's introduction is shown; when the Shop or the League is opened; and when you turn usage data on.
  • Three settings about you as a player: your level range (for example "5–9"), your preferred difficulty, and how many days after installing the game you first spent coins.
  • What the Analytics software adds by itself: a random app-instance identifier and a Firebase installation identifier, your device model, operating system and app version, your language, approximate location (such as country and city, derived by Google from your masked IP address), and when sessions start and how long they last.

Usage data is not linked to your name, email address or Versus account. We do not collect your advertising ID. Ad personalisation and Google signals are switched off for this data. Google keeps it for 14 months.

Versus (multiplayer) and the League

Versus and the League are optional and need an account. When you choose to sign in, we create an account for you and store the following in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore):

  • Account identifier — a Firebase user ID. Depending on how you sign in, it is linked to: an anonymous guest ID (no personal data); your Google account's email address and display name; or an email address and password you provide.
  • Profile — a username you choose and a short friend code, both visible to other players so they can challenge you.
  • Gameplay records ("traces") — for each Versus round you complete: the round's seed and difficulty, which answer you tapped for each question, and your answer times. This is what lets an opponent replay and compare the same round. It contains no personal information beyond your username.
  • Friends — the friend codes you add.
  • League scores — your best score on each League board, with your username and the time it was set, shown to other players on the boards.

We do not collect your contacts, phone number, photos, precise location or advertising ID.

Why we collect it

  • Usage data: to understand how the game is played (for example which modes are played and where rounds are quit) and to improve it.
  • Account data: to run Versus and the League: to identify your account, let friends find you by code, deliver challenges, show side-by-side comparisons and show the boards.

We never sell your data.

Legal bases (European Economic Area and United Kingdom)

Usage data is collected only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. Account data is processed because it is needed to provide the features you choose to use (Versus and the League).

Who processes it

Google provides Firebase and Google Analytics and processes this data on our behalf on Google Cloud infrastructure. The Firestore database is in Tel Aviv (me-west1); Google may process analytics data in other countries under its data-transfer safeguards. See Google's privacy practices at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw your consent, by writing to ilailevavi@gmail.com. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your data-protection authority.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → your account → Delete account. This immediately erases your profile, username, friend code, friends, gameplay traces, League scores and sign-in credentials.
  • By the web: if you cannot use the app, request deletion at the account-deletion page or email ilailevavi@gmail.com and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Usage data is not tied to your account. To stop it and delete what is waiting on your phone, switch off Settings → Privacy → Share usage data. Usage data already sent is deleted by Google after 14 months.

Children

LearnLand is a general-audience game for players aged 13 and over, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
