LearnLand — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

LearnLand is a geography quiz game. Single-player is fully offline and needs no account. The optional Versus (multiplayer) mode uses a backend (Google Firebase) and requires signing in. This policy explains what data that mode collects, why, and how to delete it.

Who we are

LearnLand is developed by Ilai Levavi. Contact: ilailevavi@gmail.com.

What single-player collects

Nothing leaves your device. Your settings, language, and high scores are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. Single-player makes no network requests.

What Versus (multiplayer) collects

When you choose to enter Versus and sign in, we create an account for you and store the following in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore):

  • Account identifier — a Firebase user ID. Depending on how you sign in, this is linked to: an anonymous guest ID (no personal data); your Google account's email and display name; or an email address and password you provide.
  • Profile — a username you choose and a short friend code, both visible to other players so they can challenge you.
  • Gameplay records ("traces") — for each Versus round you complete: the round's seed and difficulty, which answer you tapped for each question, and your answer times. This is what lets an opponent replay and compare the same round. It contains no personal information beyond your username.
  • Friends — friend codes you add.

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, photos, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Why we collect it

Solely to run multiplayer: to identify your account, let friends find you by code, deliver challenges, and show side-by-side comparisons. We do not sell data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is processed by Google Firebase on Google Cloud infrastructure (Firestore database region: Tel Aviv, me-west1). Google acts as our data processor; see Google's privacy practices at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → your account → Delete account. This immediately erases your profile, username, friend code, friends, gameplay traces, and sign-in credentials.
  • By the web: if you cannot use the app, request deletion at the account-deletion page or email ilailevavi@gmail.com and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Children

LearnLand is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
