Delete your LearnLand account

This page explains how to delete your LearnLand (Versus/multiplayer) account and all data associated with it. Single-player uses no account and stores nothing online.

Fastest: delete in the app

  1. Open LearnLand and tap Play → Multiplayer (or the account icon in Settings).
  2. Open your account screen.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm by typing DELETE.

This immediately and permanently erases your profile, username, friend code, friends list, all gameplay records, and your sign-in credentials.

Can't use the app? Request deletion by email

Email ilailevavi@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or include your LearnLand username and friend code. We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Everything: your Firebase Authentication credential, your public profile (username + friend code), your friends, and every gameplay trace you created (challenges and random-match recordings). Nothing is retained.
